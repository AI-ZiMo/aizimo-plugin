## Typical Traffic Patterns

- **Top subdomain**: 20K-30K clicks/month (e.g. `rule34dle.vercel.app` at 29.5K)
- **Mid tier**: 2K-10K clicks/month — most actionable for new entrants
- **Low tier**: 200-2K clicks/month — good for nichedown or learning

## Filtering Tips

- Focus on sites whose **top keyword is NOT their own brand name** — that means
  real organic search traffic, not direct/navigation traffic.
- Look for **positive change %** (>5000% means a breakout — these are gold).
- Prefer sites with **100+ keywords** and **avg rank < 10**.
